## 🧠 About me

I work on two sides of software. By day I build the business systems companies run on — multi-branch ERP, travel booking and online shops — with **Next.js, Go and SQL**. As a researcher, I study how deep-learning models read medical images, and how to make them **explain** their decisions with Grad-CAM, LIME and SHAP.

- 💼 **Software Engineer** at **Creation Plus**, Dhaka (Nov 2025 – present)
- 🤖 Currently integrating an **LLM-powered customer-chat agent** into a live e-commerce platform
- 📄 **First author** of a **Scopus Q1** journal article and author of **16 peer-reviewed** IEEE and Springer conference papers
- 🎓 B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering, **Pabna University of Science and Technology** (2025)
- 🌱 Open to **Software Engineer, Full-Stack and AI/ML Engineer** roles in Dhaka
